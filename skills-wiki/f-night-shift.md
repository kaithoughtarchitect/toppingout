# f-night-shift

Slash: `/f-night-shift`. Folder: `skills/f-night-shift/SKILL.md`.

Run several jobs one after another without you: a shift boss opens a fresh foreman for each.

**On site:** When you type /goal night shift with a list of jobs, or a number of jobs to pick: usually before you sleep.

Each job is its own autonomous run; the boss only starts it and checks the repo. Any stop ends the shift, and so does a completed topping out (the next head is yours to pick); the morning summary is one file.

**When to use it:** Before you sleep, when the next jobs are on the board and you want them built one after another without you.
