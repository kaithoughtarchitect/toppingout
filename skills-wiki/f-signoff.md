# f-signoff

Slash: `/f-signoff`. Folder: `skills/f-signoff/SKILL.md`.

Close a merged job: check every requirement passed, save the lessons, file the job away.

**On site:** After the merge: closes the job only when every requirement has passed, with nothing put off for later.

Then come the dev report, the choice of the next job, the board update and the handover, in that order. At the board's head it tops the project out instead: a topping out report, the frozen board, and candidate next heads for you to pick from.

**When to use it:** Right after a job's pull request is merged and the merge is confirmed.
