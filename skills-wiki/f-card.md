# f-card

Slash: `/f-card`. Folder: `skills/f-card/SKILL.md`.

Save one lesson worth keeping as a Logbook card.

**On site:** The Logbook (the project's memory of lessons): any time something worth keeping is learned, during a job or not.

Each card is a lesson (why), a method (how) or a record of what was built, written from the templates in `foreman/logbook/entries/`, with its evidence.

**When to use it:** Whenever you learn something worth remembering for future jobs.
