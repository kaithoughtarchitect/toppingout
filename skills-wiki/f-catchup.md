# f-catchup

Slash: `/f-catchup`. Folder: `skills/f-catchup/SKILL.md`.

Write Logbook cards for past jobs that never got any.

**On site:** The Logbook: by itself at every close, for up to three past jobs not yet looked at; by hand to fill it from many past jobs at once, when it shows you what's missing and waits for a yes.

Checks each lesson against its job before writing it, and skips jobs with nothing to learn.

**When to use it:** Automatically at every close, for up to three past jobs without lessons; by hand to seed many at once.
