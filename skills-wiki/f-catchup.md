# f-catchup

Slash: `/f-catchup`. Folder: `skills/f-catchup/SKILL.md`.

Write Logbook cards for past jobs that never got any.

**On site:** The Logbook: once to fill it from past jobs, or after a gap. It shows you what's missing and waits for a yes.

Checks each lesson against its job before writing it, and skips jobs with nothing to learn.

**When to use it:** When past jobs finished without anyone saving their lessons.
