# f-distill

Slash: `/f-distill`. Folder: `skills/f-distill/SKILL.md`.

Turn the most important lessons into firm rules, with your approval.

**On site:** The Logbook: every 10th close writes a preview of the lessons ready to become rules; you apply it whenever you like. Lessons about the product become rules in the repo's own instructions; lessons about the method become Foreman rules.

Shows you the proposed rules first and writes nothing until you approve them.

**When to use it:** When the close report says lessons are waiting in the preview, or whenever you like.
