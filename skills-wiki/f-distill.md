# f-distill

Slash: `/f-distill`. Folder: `skills/f-distill/SKILL.md`.

Turn the most important lessons into firm rules, with your approval.

**On site:** The Logbook: now and then. Lessons about the product become rules in the repo's own instructions; lessons about the method become Foreman rules.

**When to use it:** Now and then, when some lessons keep coming up and deserve to become firm rules.
