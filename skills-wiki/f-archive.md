# f-archive

Slash: `/f-archive`. Folder: `skills/f-archive/SKILL.md`.

Move old Logbook cards to the archive when there are too many, keeping a summary set.

**On site:** The Logbook: [[f-shift]] runs it by itself once there are more than 250 cards.

Moves older cards to `foreman/logbook/archived/` and keeps a short summary set, as `ARCHIVE-SYSTEM.md` describes.

**When to use it:** When the Logbook passes 250 cards (f-shift does it for you).
