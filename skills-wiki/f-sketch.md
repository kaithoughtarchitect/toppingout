# f-sketch

Slash: `/f-sketch`. Folder: `skills/f-sketch/SKILL.md`.

Write down a rough idea and its open questions, before anything is decided.

**On site:** Before a job, the very first step: when an idea is worth keeping but not ready to decide.

A notes file, not a plan: what's agreed so far, the open questions and the options turned down. When it's ready it moves on to [[f-survey]].

**When to use it:** When you have an idea worth writing down but you're not ready to decide anything about it yet.
