ItA new board

An empty plot for the new head, and the job loop starts again.

Every word is yours. Nothing passes go, open the PR, greploop or merge without you, and the next ticket waits for your yes.

It moves on without you only on an explicit grant: an autonomous run for one job, or a night shift. Any other standing goal grants nothing.

It asks you only where a step is yours: the acts RULES.md lists, a deferral, the next ticket. Technical choices it makes on evidence, and writes down what it turned down.

It still stops and waits, one line saying why, on: the same failure red after two repair rounds; a security finding or an unresolved critical one; Greptile under 5/5 after three rounds; spend reaching your cap. After a stop it goes on only on your word.

Your goal never covers tracker moves, messages, edits outside the ticket’s files, migrations, a force push, a fourth Greptile round, spend over the cap, or starting the next job.

The shift ends at any stop in a job, at a topping out, at the cap, or when a job passes 16 hours. The night shift, in full ↓

Why Topping Out

Site ruleDo not distract the builder. It only builds what’s drawn.

Named for the day a building's last beam goes on. Here, it's the day every job on a board is done: the project tops out, and the next one starts from it.

Build only what is drawn

Cheaper

The foreman decides, so a cheap model can build.

Inspection zone

Checked end to end

Two inspections and a checklist. “Done” has to be proven.

Works continue overnight

Runs the night shift

Hand it a list at night. Wake up to merged jobs.

Site diary kept at all times

Nothing forgotten

A new session picks up where the last one stopped.

A failed check stops the shift, so nothing broken merges. 3 jobs, auto-pick lets it choose. How it works →

Night shift rulesAll workers · every night
  • Any problem stops the whole shift, with one line saying why. A usage limit only pauses it.
  • Done means merged in the repo, never "done" in the chat.
  • Nothing outside the ticket: no messages, migrations, force pushes or spend over the cap.
  • Start small: two or three jobs a night.

How a card is made5 / 12

From lesson to card

skills/f-card/ and /f-signoff step 3.2

A lesson is found, checked for a twin, shown to you, then filed.

Who
/f-card any time; /f-signoff at the close.
Never
Filed without your yes.
  1. 1MineThe job's key discoveries, its work-order reviews, its inspections and the dev report's learnings. Or one lesson, mid-job.
  2. 2Type itINSIGHT for a gotcha or principle, METHOD for a repeatable procedure, BUILD for a record of what was built. Each has its template.
  3. 3Check for a twinSearch the Logbook for the same lesson. Over 80% overlap: the evidence goes on the old card instead.
  4. 4Score itEvery section filled, the evidence, a concrete example. Confidence from evidence: one instance 75%, two or more 85%, measured 95%. Under 75 of 100, it goes back.
  5. 5Show youThe whole card as a preview. Nothing is written until you say yes.
  6. 6File itThe card file, a row in the index, its links, and the counts recounted from the files.

Site officeQuery 01 of 09

Why not one agent for everything?

The agent that made a mistake would be the one judging it.

ProofSo an AI from another company checks the work cold. It once caught a race the foreman had passed.

Source: Foreman Playbook, "The one idea"

Site officeQuery 02 of 09

Why not just write better prompts?

A prompt asks. A check proves.

ProofEach test must fail before the code and pass after it, and every requirement needs evidence before the job closes.

Source: METHOD.md sections 7 and 10

Site officeQuery 03 of 09

Why does it need my OK?

Merging and pushing reach beyond your machine.

ProofThey wait for you. On a night shift, your /goal line is the OK, for those jobs only.

Source: RULES.md, "Acts that need the owner's exact word"

Site officeQuery 04 of 09

Isn't it slow and costly?

More steps per job. Less of your time.

ProofA cheap model on a flat subscription does the building, the checks catch problems first, and jobs run while you sleep.

Source: the Requirements page; the night shift above

Site officeQuery 05 of 09

Am I locked in?

No. It is plain text files in your own repo.

ProofStop using it, and the plans, the lessons and the board stay.

Source: SETUP.md, "What Topping Out adds to your repo"

Site officeQuery 06 of 09

What if it breaks at night?

The shift stops, and says why.

ProofA red check, a failed walkthrough or a security finding halts it.

Source: the night shift above

Site officeQuery 07 of 09

What if the builder cuts corners?

Its report is never taken on its word.

ProofEvery requirement needs a pass with evidence, or the job does not close.

Source: METHOD.md section 10, the acceptance ledger

Site officeQuery 08 of 09

Will it fit my project?

Yes. It adds files and overwrites nothing.

ProofSetup adds one foreman/ folder and a short profile of your project.

Source: SETUP.md section 1

Expected to, not yet verified.

/f-setup

Update later: replaces the files your repo didn't adapt and lists the ones it did; it waits while a job is open.

Prefer to copy the files by hand?

This is the installer from SETUP.md section 1, the same on Windows, macOS and Linux. Fill in the two paths, run the dry run, then the install. It copies only what is missing and writes an install receipt.

PKG="<path to the Topping Out package>"; REPO="<path to your records repo>"
node "$PKG/skills/f-setup/install.mjs" --repo "$REPO" --dry-run   # what it would copy, and what it keeps
node "$PKG/skills/f-setup/install.mjs" --repo "$REPO"             # copy what's missing, never overwrite

Then write the repo profile (templates/REPO-PROFILE.md) and the test adapter, and check the install:

python .claude/skills/f-board/test_render_board.py

Enforced, or only instructed from the README

Most of the method is instructions the agents follow; a few checks are scripts that refuse.

  • Gated acts (push, merge, messages) wait for your wordinstruction
    What it doesn’t stop

    (RULES.md), plus your coding tool's own permission prompts. The package ships no hook

    An agent with push rights and pre-allowed commands can push; nothing in the package blocks it technically. SETUP.md §5 suggests a hook for migrations only, and SETUP.md §7 asks you to pre-allow git push, gh pr create and gh pr merge for an autonomous run.

  • What merged is what was revieweda check the foreman runs
    What it doesn’t stop

    The merged commit's tree must equal the reviewed and tested tree (METHOD.md §10 step 3), and skills/f-signoff/endgame.mjs verify-merge refuses a close whose merge isn't on the main branch

    The tree comparison is not scripted, and verify-merge checks only that the merge commit is on main (and runs because /f-signoff calls it); an edit after the final re-run invalidates the acceptance and is caught only if the foreman re-runs, as the rules require.

  • Nothing new starts until a closed job's end game is donea script the skills run
    What it doesn’t stop

    endgame.mjs keeps a lock file; the planning skills and work order 1 run its check and stop while it exists

    An agent that skips the check call isn't stopped.

  • The review is independenta script, when used
    What it doesn’t stop

    scripts/cross-review-dispatch.mjs seats the reviewer on another vendor's CLI, in a separate process, from a brief (/pack-review)

    The foreman writes the brief, so it chooses what the reviewer is pointed at and told (the reviewer can also read the checkout, read-only); seats exist only for Claude and codex foremen; this is separation, not adversarial isolation.

  • The whole ask is doneinstruction plus a record
    What it doesn’t stop

    The acceptance ledger lists every requirement and check with its evidence, and /f-signoff refuses unless every row is PASS. A change to the ask is recorded in the owner's own words (METHOD.md §2)

    The ledger is written and checked by agents; a wrong PASS is caught only by the reviews or by you.

  • Builder names stay out of the recordsa script the foreman runs
    What it doesn’t stop

    skills/f-signoff/names-check.mjs checks every staged record at the records push and exits non-zero on a hit

    It runs only when the foreman calls it at the records push; it checks only the names in your names file, and skips allowed contexts and rendered board pages.
