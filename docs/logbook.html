A lesson or a trap: why something happens.

INSIGHTINSIGHT_20260220_REAIMED_PROBE_HIDES_GAP
cluster: testingimpact: HIGHconfidence: 85an example card
  1. OverviewA mutation probe that stays GREEN means no test covers that guard. Re-aiming the probe until it bites hides the gap instead of closing it.
  2. ImpactA guard ships with no test behind it, while the report says every probe bites.
  3. Pattern StructureSignal: a probe stays GREEN. Cause: the builder moves the probe somewhere a test does catch. Result: the probe bites and the missing test stays missing.
  4. Anti-PatternTreating GREEN as a broken probe and fixing the probe. In one job, a well-shaped forged value was refused only by the guard, and no test covered it.
  5. EvidenceTwo builders, in two jobs, re-aimed a GREEN probe; both times it hid a missing test (METHOD.md section 7).
  6. ApplicationsOn GREEN the builder reports and stops (work order rule 7). Before sending a work order, the foreman checks every probe anchor still matches once.
How sure
Confidence comes from the evidence: 75 for one instance, 85 for two or more, 95 when it was measured.

The cards · the check4 / 5
75

How a card is checked

out of 100 to pass

Every card is scored before you see it. The score catches thin cards; the checks are what matter.

  • start at 50Every card.
  • +20Every section is filled in.
  • +15The evidence is strong: confidence or success rate of 80 or more, or a BUILD card that cites its PR or commit.
  • +10It has a concrete example.
  • +5It has a counter-example (INSIGHT cards only).
  • 75 to passBelow that, you see the score and what is missing, and choose to fix it or file it anyway.

Your wordYou see the whole card first. Nothing is written until you say yes.

02

/f-card makes it a card

What happens
It picks a type, checks the Logbook for a twin, scores the card and shows it to you. With your yes it is filed: the card, its row in the index, its links.
Done by
/f-card/f-signoff

Your wordYou see the card first. Nothing is filed until you say yes.

  1. 1MineThe job's key discoveries, its work-order reviews, its inspections and the dev report's learnings. Or one lesson, mid-job.
  2. 2Type itINSIGHT for a gotcha or principle, METHOD for a repeatable procedure, BUILD for a record of what was built. Each has its template.
  3. 3Check for a twinSearch the Logbook for the same lesson. Over 80% overlap: the evidence goes on the old card instead.
  4. 4Score itEvery section filled, the evidence, a concrete example. Confidence from evidence: one instance 75%, two or more 85%, measured 95%. Under 75 of 100, it goes back.
  5. 5Show youThe whole card as a preview. Nothing is written until you say yes.
  6. 6File itThe card file, a row in the index, its links, and the counts recounted from the files.
