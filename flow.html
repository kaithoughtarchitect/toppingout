Topping Out·the route, start to finish

From an idea to the next job

A foreman plans and inspects, a builder builds from work orders, readers from another model family check cold, and you give the word for every act that needs it. Seven stages; the build loop repeats once per task, and the whole route once per job.

A plan becomes a project

Once per big piece of work. 8 steps

  1. 01

    Bring the idea

    What you want built, in your words. Not ready yet? Capture it with its open questions kept.

    /f-sketchoptional
    Owner
  2. 02

    Survey it

    Settle every open question against the code, so nothing is left to guess. Aloud if you like; a council can settle a fork you hand it.

    /f-survey/model-council/speakcouncils, voice optional
    Foreman
  3. 03

    Write the blueprint

    One decision-complete plan: locked decisions, acceptance (VPs), tasks.

    /f-blueprintforeman/blueprints/ready/
    Foreman
  4. 04

    Cold read of the blueprint

    One to five seats from other model families read it cold, in parallel. Findings are merged, never voted. It writes the gate line.

    /f-plancheck
    Inspector
  5. 05

    Settle every finding

    Each finding applied to the blueprint or answered in it, its fate in the findings table. No gate line, no cut.

    Foreman
  6. 06

    Cut it into jobs

    One ticket per job, one PR each. Owner decisions become their own jobs. Every VP carried. Shown to you first.

    /f-schedulesimplicity-critic
    Foreman
  7. 07

    Approve the cut

    Writing the project and publishing the board are separate words.

    Owner
    Your word“go · publish”
  8. 08

    Board v1

    BOARD.json renders the fishbone page you follow. The first eligible job is marked next.

    /f-boardforeman/projects/<slug>/
    Foreman

Start a job

Every job, one ticket. 6 steps

  1. 09

    Open a fresh session

    Paste the opener the last job left beside its handover. The first job on a board has its own opener.

    foreman/handovers/OPENER-JOB-<N>.txt
    Owner
  2. 10

    Stand up as foreman

    Loads the role, the rules and the playbook in full, the lessons that apply to this job, and checks the project's board (none yet: start it with init).

    /f-shift/f-searchREPO-PROFILE.md
    Foreman
  3. 11

    Site walk

    Answer the questions cold, then check the key. Measure the machine. Read the live ticket. Report every owed decision. Stop.

    METHOD §2
    Foreman
  4. 12

    Pre-flight

    No product writes: proves Bash, git, writing outside its folder, the tools and its permissions. READY or BLOCKED.

    BUILDER-PREFLIGHT.txt/f-complyf-comply optional
    Builder
  5. 13

    Say go

    Choosing the ticket is not authorising the build.

    Owner
    Your word“go”
  6. 14

    Mark it started

    The job goes in progress on the board.

    $ render_board.py start
    Foreman

Setup and the plan

Before any product code. 3 steps

  1. 15

    Work order 1: setup and t0

    Cut the worktree by name, write the job record, capture t0 on every gate. No code.

    WORK-ORDER-1-SETUP.txt
    Builder
  2. 16

    Scoping

    Settle every design fork with evidence, and record what was rejected.

    SCOPING.md
    Foreman
  3. 17

    The plan and gate 1

    Requirements verbatim, tasks, VPs, the tests that must fail first. Then a cold read, the same way as the blueprint.

    PLAN.md/f-plancheck
    Foreman
3

The build loop

One work order per task, until every task is built. 4 steps

  1. 18

    Write the work order

    One verifiable outcome. Decisions labelled D-, exact commands, a pinned HEAD. Tick the pre-send checklist.

    WORK-ORDER.txt
    Foreman
  2. 19

    Pass the pointer

    One line: read file X, lines A to B. Or the foreman sends it over the pane link and a background watcher wakes it at the stop.

    /orca-pi-connectpane link optional, Orca
    Owner
  3. 20

    Build it

    Tests fail first, then build. Hostile inputs, coverage, probes, G1 to G6, every touched module's tests. One commit, captures, a report, stop.

    Builder
  4. 21

    Review it

    Disk before words. Run the new tests on the old code. Probe it yourself. Attribute every difference. Update the job record.

    WORK-ORDER-REVIEW.md
    Foreman

  5. Repeat per task. Repairs go test-first into the next work order. A GREEN probe means a missing test: stop. A HALT is recorded on the board.

4

Inspections

After the last task. The owner stays out of it. 9 steps

  1. 22

    Base versus head

    Every test that depends on a changed module, and the wider suites, at base and at head, through the repo's test adapter. Anything red only at head: stop.

    BASE-VS-HEAD.txtTEST-ADAPTERS.md
    Builder
  2. 23

    Gate 2

    Every commit against its review, final captures against t0, the foreman's own base-vs-head, the one safety fact.

    GATE2-REVIEW.md
    Foreman
  3. 24

    Lens inspection

    The foreman runs it in its own session over the job's range, lenses as fresh subagents, findings only. No PR exists yet. QUICK jobs skip it.

    /f-inspectreview lensesLENS-INSPECTION.md
    Foreman
  4. 25

    Check every finding

    Reproduce each against the code; accept, reject or partly accept. Only what's accepted goes back.

    ADJUDICATION.md
    Foreman
  5. 26

    Fix what was accepted

    Test-first repair work orders, reviewed like any other.

    WORK-ORDER.txt
    Builder
  6. 27

    Pack the review

    One blind brief: goal, constraints verbatim, non-goals, the diff, absolute paths. Nothing names the builder.

    /pack-reviewPACK-REVIEW.md
    Foreman
  7. 28

    Cross-model review

    A fresh session of the other model family reviews it cold. Carried by hand, or the bundled dispatcher runs it headless.

    /xreview$ cross-review-dispatch.mjs
    Inspector
  8. 29

    Adjudicate

    Every claim reproduced first. Accepted fixes become work orders; security and concurrency fixes go back to the reviewer.

    /adjudicate
    Foreman
  9. 30

    Final re-run and the ledger

    Captures, probes and base-vs-head again at the final head. Every requirement and VP passes, or there is no PR.

    ACCEPTANCE-LEDGER.md
    Foreman
5

PR and merge

Outward acts: each one needs your word. 6 steps

  1. 31

    PR body

    What changed, acceptance, proof, limitations, rollback, the review record. Scanned for builder names.

    PR-BODY.md
    Foreman
  2. 32

    Open the PR

    Pushed and opened; you watch it in the browser. Opening is not merging.

    Owner
    Your word“open the PR”
  3. 33

    Greptile rounds

    Greptile scores the open PR out of 5. Its findings are checked like any inspection, the builder fixes what's accepted, the tests and sign-off re-run, then the push. Three rounds at most, then it stops for you.

    /f-greploop
    Foreman
    Your word“greploop”
  4. 34

    PR description check

    Before you're asked to merge: the PR's description is re-read against the final code and results, and anything stale is rewritten.

    Foreman
  5. 35

    Merge

    Then the merge's tree is checked against the tree that was reviewed.

    Owner
    Your word“merge”
  6. 36

    Clean up

    Once the tree check passes: delete the merged branch and remove the job's worktrees, unasked. Only the tracker transition waits for your word.

    Foreman
    Your word“tracker transition”
6

Close and the next job

Then back to stage 1 while a next job is confirmed; at the head, top out and stop. 9 steps

  1. 37

    Close the job

    Only on a merge that's really on main, and only when every requirement passed. Then the record, Logbook cards, the archive, and the end-game lock opens: nothing new starts until the steps below are done.

    /f-signoffforeman/completed-jobs/
    Foreman
  2. 38

    Dev report

    The day's lesson, and the foreman's own scorecard.

    DEV-REPORT.md
    Foreman
  3. 39

    Next-ticket decision

    The foreman reads the board and recommends the next ticket, naming the board version and what picked it.

    NEXT-TICKET-DECISION.md$ render_board.py next
    Foreman
  4. 40

    Confirm the next job

    Say yes, or pick another. Under a standing goal the foreman decides and records it.

    Owner
  5. 41

    Ground investigation

    Four research agents, one angle each, plus the repo's framework check: the next survey starts from what they found.

    GROUND-INVESTIGATION.md
    Foreman
  6. 42

    Board close

    One close event: merged plus the confirmed next job, one version, republished to the same link.

    /f-board$ render_board.py close --next
    Foreman
  7. 43

    Shift handover and opener

    A session brief becomes the shift handover and its answer key, plus the next session's opener as a file beside them, ready to paste.

    handoff-builderforeman/handovers/
    Foreman
  8. 44

    Top out (when the head is reached)

    The other ending, when every job is done: close the last job, top the board out (frozen as the project's record), write the topping out report, then three to five candidate next heads instead of a handover and opener. You pick a head when you're ready and the cycle starts again.

    TOPPING-OUT-REPORT.mdNEXT-HEADS.md$ render_board.py topout
    Foreman
  9. 45

    Push the records

    The end-game lock clears. Merge main into the records branch (never a rebase), check only records differ, push.

    Foreman

  10. Back to stage 1 with the next job, while one is confirmed. When every job is merged or resolved, the board tops out and the work stops: only you pick the next head.

The Logbook, now and then

/f-card for a lesson mid-job. /f-dream to merge duplicates and find repeated mistakes. /f-distill to promote the best cards into rules, on your word. /f-archive past 250 cards; ruled-out options always stay in view.

Form B: an audition

For a builder on trial, stage 2 changes: the builder writes the plan itself as plain steps (WORK-ORDER-2-PLAN.txt) and the foreman judges it at gate 1 (GATE1-REVIEW.md). Everything else on the route stays the same.
